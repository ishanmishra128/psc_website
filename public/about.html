About Us

Building bridges of understanding and solidarity at UW-Madison

Our Mission

The Palestine Solidarity Committee's primary objective is to promote Palestinian culture and advocate for the Palestinian cause at UW-Madison. We intend to foster awareness, build relationships, and create a space for Palestine on campus.

Through educational events, cultural celebrations, and community engagement, we strive to create meaningful dialogue and understanding about Palestinian history, culture, and the ongoing struggle for justice and human rights.

PSC's creation is in direct response to UW-Madison's continued attempts to silence Palestinian activism on campus. We refuse to let UW absolve itself of its connection to the ongoing genocide and occupation of Palestine. PSC is committed to promoting Palestinian culture on campus, and keeping the Palestinian cause strong at UW through organizing and solidarity.

Our Purpose

Promoting Palestinian culture and raising awareness about Palestinian history and current events

Our Values

Justice, solidarity, education, and building lasting connections within our community

Our Community

An inclusive space for all students interested in learning about and supporting Palestine

What We Do

Cultural Events

We host cultural nights, cooking workshops, and art exhibitions that celebrate Palestinian heritage and traditions.

Educational Programs

Through film screenings, speaker series, and panel discussions, we provide education about Palestinian history and current affairs.

Community Building

We create spaces for students to connect, share experiences, and build lasting relationships centered around solidarity and justice.

Fundraising

We organize fundraising events to support humanitarian causes and educational programs that benefit Palestinian communities.
